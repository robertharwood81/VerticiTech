The problem

Most digital transformations stall on the shop floor.

70–80%of transformation efforts fail · McKinsey, Deloitte, BCG

The software usually works. What breaks down is everything around it: knowledge gaps between shifts, silos between IT and operations, and frontline teams who were never asked what they needed.

When operators see a new tablet as surveillance or extra paperwork, adoption quietly fails, and the pilot never scales.

Our approach

Technology and culture, handled together.

We combine hands-on IT and connected worker experience with applied anthropology, so the system you deploy fits the way your people really work.

Our story

It started with a few paper routes.

Inside a large manufacturer, Jeremy was asked to digitize a handful of paper rounds so frontline data would be easier to use. That small project turned out to touch everything on the shop floor: communication, data analysis, training, knowledge transfer, maintenance tracking, and above all, employee engagement.

He partnered early with Augmentir, back when “connected worker” wasn't yet an industry term. Rolling out a transformation program from inside the IT silo was hard. Jeremy had one unexpected advantage: he'd married a cultural anthropologist. Jessica joined as a consultant and worked from the frontline up, uncovering hidden stigmas and starting the conversations that drove adoption.

Technology alone doesn't drive transformation. People do.

Vertici exists to bring that lesson to other manufacturers: make work smarter, more engaging, and more human, with happier employees and stronger results to show for it.
