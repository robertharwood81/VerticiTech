Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Vertici LLC collects when you visit verticitech.com, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

We collect very little information through this website.

  • Information you send us. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share, such as your name, email address, company, and message.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records limited technical details when you visit, such as your browser type, the pages you request, and an approximate location based on your IP address.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information through this website.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website
  • To understand general, non-identifying usage trends

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies to keep the site secure and working properly. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Its handling of that information is described in Cloudflare's privacy policy. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmitting or storing data online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other websites

This website may link to sites we do not operate. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children's privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
