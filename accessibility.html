Accessibility Statement

Last updated October 12, 2026

Vertici LLC wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, regardless of technology or ability. We treat accessibility as ongoing work, not a finished task.

Our approach

We have not completed a formal audit against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, 2.1, or 2.2, and we do not claim conformance with any specific standard. We do design and build the site with accessibility best practices in mind, including:

  • Clear headings and a logical page structure
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
  • Readable type, sufficient color contrast, and support for light and dark display settings
  • Layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and larger screens
  • Reduced motion for visitors who have turned it on in their device settings

Ongoing efforts

We review the site periodically and make improvements as part of routine updates, with continued attention to structure and navigation, readability, keyboard access, and contrast and text sizing.

Limitations and alternatives

Some content, particularly content provided by third-party tools such as scheduling services, may not be fully accessible. If you run into a barrier, please let us know. We will do our best to provide the information you need in another format.
