OUR STORY

Our journey began inside a large manufacturer with what seemed like a simple request: digitize a few paper routes so frontline data would be easier to use. That small project touched everything on the shop floor: communication, data analysis, training, knowledge transfer, maintenance tracking, and above all, employee engagement.

Back then, Digital Transformation and Connected Worker weren't industry buzzwords yet. Jeremy partnered early with Augmentir, and rolling out a transformation program from inside the IT silo was hard. He had one unexpected advantage: he'd married a cultural anthropologist. Jessica joined as a consultant and worked from the frontline up, uncovering hidden stigmas and starting the conversations that drove adoption.

What We Learned

Technology alone doesn't drive transformation. People do. That experience taught us how to bridge gaps, build collaboration, and create systems that empower employees.

Our Mission

We help organizations connect people, streamline processes, and build cultures that lead to happier employees and stronger results. Our goal is simple: make work smarter, more engaging, and more human.
